Cover trafficEmit dummy deposits on a random (Poisson) schedule so an observer can't read when you really send. Noise, not a guarantee — it costs constant background bandwidth. A higher rate hides more but costs more bandwidth.

Security & duress

Decoy and duress hide WHICH account is real, but disk forensics can still tell an extra account is there — strong against being forced to unlock, not full deniability. The hidden container is the exception: it’s indistinguishable from random, so even its existence is deniable.
